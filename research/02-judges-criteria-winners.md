## 2. Ideology and thesis: what HashKey wants built

### 2.1 HashKey Group positioning

### 2.2 The agent-economy thesis

- **[FACT]** April 21 2026, Web3 Festival: HashKey Group released its 3rd Web3 Economy whitepaper, "On-Chain Finance and Tokenization in the Era of the Agent Economy", presented by Dr. Xiao Feng.
  Key claims: economic activity shifts to "high-frequency collaboration between humans and Agents, as well as Agent-to-Agent interactions"; stablecoins are "gradually evolving into the cash settlement layer of the on-chain financial system".
  Sources: https://yellow.com/press-releases/hashkey-releases-web3-report-on-tokenization-in-the-ai-agent-economy and https://www.finanznachrichten.de/nachrichten-2026-04/68254236-hashkey-group-releases-3rd-web3-economy-whitepaper-reconstructing-on-chain-finance-and-tokenization-infrastructure-for-the-ai-agent-economy-era-008.htm
- **[FACT]** April 20 2026: HashKey Chain launched HSP (HashKey Settlement Protocol), an extension of Google's AP2, for AI agents, cross-border e-commerce and physical enterprises.
  Features: native HTTP interaction for agent payments, A2A payments, stablecoin micro cross-border settlement below $0.01, licensed verification nodes, on-chain AML, privacy-preserving but auditable transactions.
  Li Chen (CEO, On-Chain Business Unit): HSP is "not merely the release of a single product"; vision of "agent-driven economics".
  Early deployments: eCandle off-grid solar micropayments, Lingchu robots paying for data APIs.

- **[FACT]** Oct 2025: HashKey Group joined Google's AP2 partner ecosystem, framed as one of the first compliant digital financial institutions in APAC to do so, aiming to explore compliant AP2 use in "stablecoin payments, on-chain identity, and on-chain settlement for AI agents".

### 3.2 Reconstructed rubric (inference)

This is our best estimate, built from the signals above and typical DoraHacks sponsor-judged events.

| Criterion | Est. weight | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Completeness and technical maturity (working mainnet demo, end to end) | 25% | "strong completeness, technical maturity", mainnet gate |
| Ecosystem fit and official product usage (HSP depth, HSK Chain, CCIP, token contracts) | 20% + bonus | "extra points" clause, HSP mandatory |
| Innovation (novel agent/A2A mechanism, not a generic payment link) | 20% | "innovation" named on page, Taichu criteria |
| Business value and commercial prospects (real customer, fee model, market) | 20% | Taichu "commercial prospects", HashKey Capital role, winners pattern |
| Compliance, security and risk controls (spend limits, AML/KYT hooks, audit trail) | 10% | HSP design, whitepaper, ZKID track, in-house legal team offer |
| Pitch and Q&A quality | 5% | Demo Day "pitches + Q&A" format |

- **[INFERENCE]** Two gates come before scoring: mainnet deployment, and being selected for a pitch slot (in-person priority).
- **[INFERENCE]** HSP usage likely functions as both a gate (payments track) and a differentiator (depth of integration).

## 5. India angle

### 5.1 Facts

- **[FACT]** Crypto (VDA) gains are taxed at a flat 30% with no loss set-off, and 1% TDS under Section 194S applies to VDA transfers above Rs 10,000 per year (Rs 50,000 for specified persons).
  Source: https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/crypto-taxation-india-vda-tax-30-percent-tds-guide/
- **[FACT]** Stablecoins have no dedicated Indian law and are treated as VDAs for tax and AML; one source says the Finance Act brings crypto-assets including stablecoins explicitly into VDA scope from April 1 2026, with exchange user-level reporting duties.
  Sources: https://www.plasma.org/learn/tools/stablecoin-regulation-map/india and https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/stablecoin-b-2-b-payments-india-offramp-compliance
- **[FACT]** The RBI remains opposed: in July 2026 it told a parliamentary panel VDAs pose monetary-stability risks; the government discussion paper has been repeatedly delayed; a Finance Ministry hearing was set for Sept 16 2026.
  Sources: https://www.cryptowire.in/blog/rbi-doubles-down-on-crypto-prohibition-as-parliamentary-panel-report-nears/ , https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/04/01/indias-crypto-policy-delayed-again-as-rbi-blocks-discussion-paper/ , https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/08/indias-crypto-law-nears-turning-point-with-sept-16-finance-ministry-hearing/
- **[FACT]** India already has agentic payments on UPI: NPCI, Razorpay and OpenAI launched an "Agentic Payments" pilot (Oct 2025) letting ChatGPT buy within preset limits using UPI Reserve Pay and UPI Circle; Cashfree also launched agentic payments for merchants.
  Sources: https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/tech-today/news/story/razorpay-npci-openai-launch-pilot-for-agentic-payments-where-ai-powered-shopping-meets-upi-497545-2025-10-09 and https://fintechnews.sg/120057/fintech-india/razorpay-agentic-payments/

### 5.2 Pitch angles that resonate (inference)

- **[INFERENCE]** Do not pitch "crypto replaces UPI" to an Indian panel; UPI already won domestic retail, and RBI hostility makes that a red flag for HashKey's compliance team.
- **[INFERENCE]** Pitch the gap UPI cannot fill: cross-border.
  Indian freelancers, SaaS exporters, agencies and AI-agent startups get paid from abroad slowly and expensively (SWIFT, PayPal fees, FIRC paperwork).
  Agents buying foreign APIs, GPU and data also have no clean rail.
  Stablecoin settlement over HSP, with a licensed off-ramp pattern (like ONERWAY), is the honest wedge.
- **[INFERENCE]** Mirror the UPI mental model for agents: "UPI Reserve Pay for AI agents, but cross-border and programmable", with mandates, per-agent budgets, revocation.
  Indian judges understand UPI mandates instantly, and HSP is itself AP2 mandate-based.
- **[INFERENCE]** Show compliance as a feature: TDS-aware ledger exports (1% TDS, 30% gains reporting), KYT/AML screening, audit trail per agent action, and a non-custodial design.
  This speaks to both the Indian reality and HashKey's "compliant by design" identity.
- **[INFERENCE]** India's AI-agent startup scene (and the ATLAS / Apna Coding developer audience) is a natural first customer segment: agent builders who need to charge per call or pay other agents.

## 6. Pitch recommendations (ranked)

1. **Be HSP-native, end to end, on mainnet, live in the room.**
   Show an agent paying another agent through HSP on HashKey Chain mainnet with the explorer tx on screen, and pitch in person in Mumbai (priority for selection).
   Use every "official product" you credibly can (HSP SDK, HashKey Chain token contracts, CCIP if cross-chain matters) and name them on one slide for the bonus points.
2. **Frame it as infrastructure for the agent economy, in HashKey's own words.**
   Reference the April 2026 whitepaper ("Agent-to-Agent interactions", stablecoins as the "cash settlement layer") and HSP's AP2 lineage.
   Position Payminto as the merchant and agent settlement gateway on top of HSP, the role AllScale played when it won Japan, plus x402 compatibility.
3. **Lead with one real business workflow, not a feature list.**
   Every past payments winner solved a concrete job (invoicing, payroll, receivables, commerce).
   Suggested: Indian agent startup or freelancer gets paid cross-border by a client's agent in stablecoin via HSP, with automatic invoice, receipt, and fiat off-ramp path.
4. **Make compliance and control visible.**
   Per-agent spend mandates and limits, revocation, KYT/AML screening hook, attestation or on-chain receipt per payment, TDS-ready export.
   This is what the two HashKey judges and their legal team will probe in Q&A.
5. **End with the company, not the hack.**
   One slide with customer, fee model (for example 0% gateway fee, revenue from agent-side value add), traction plan on HashKey Chain, and what you would do with HashKey Capital support.
   The prize is small; the incubation, investment and compliance help are the real upside, and judges score "commercial prospects".
